FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Your Kashmir cab booking questions, answered

What is the best way to arrange an airport pickup?

Send your arrival date, flight number, landing time, phone number, guest count and luggage details. Your written confirmation should show the pickup point and contact details.

Which vehicle is suitable for a family of five?

An Innova or similar spacious vehicle is often comfortable, but luggage volume matters. Share the number and size of your bags so we can recommend an appropriate option.

Can I book local sightseeing or a one-way cab?

Yes. We arrange local sightseeing, airport transfers, one-way routes, round trips and multi-day plans, subject to vehicle and route availability.

What does a multi-day cab quotation include?

Your quotation will identify the vehicle, driver, agreed itinerary, limits, fuel, driver allowance, tolls, parking and taxes as applicable. Anything excluded will be stated separately.

Do local taxi rules apply at major destinations?

Some sightseeing zones around Gulmarg, Pahalgam and Sonamarg may require locally authorised vehicles. We flag known local-transfer requirements in your plan.

Can I request a child seat or accessibility support?

Add the requirement before booking. Availability must be confirmed in writing and may depend on the assigned vehicle.

How far ahead should I request a vehicle?

Earlier is better during spring, summer, autumn foliage, Christmas and New Year periods. Last-minute requests can still be checked against live availability.

How do I confirm and pay?

Your quotation will state the advance, remaining balance, accepted methods and cancellation terms. Pay only using details in an official Waadi Kashmir Travels confirmation.
