Terms, booking and cancellation policy

Last updated: 11 August 2026

Quotes and confirmation

Website prices are indicative unless a quotation states otherwise. A booking is confirmed only after written confirmation and receipt of the stated deposit. Availability can change until confirmation.

Payments

Payment stages, accepted methods and due dates will be listed in your quotation. Guests are responsible for bank or payment-provider charges unless agreed otherwise.

Changes and cancellations

Cancellation charges depend on travel dates and supplier rules. The exact schedule supplied in your accepted quotation takes precedence. Changes may cause revised hotel, transport or activity costs.

Travel conditions

Weather, road closures, safety restrictions, flight disruption and other circumstances outside our control may require itinerary changes. We will provide reasonable assistance, but additional costs may remain the traveller’s responsibility.

Guest responsibilities

Guests must provide accurate details, carry valid identification and permits, follow local laws and disclose relevant accessibility or medical needs before confirmation. Travel insurance is strongly recommended.
