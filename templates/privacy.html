Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 August 2026

Information we collect

We collect the details you submit through enquiry and booking forms, such as your name, email, phone number, travel dates, group size and trip preferences. Basic technical information may be collected by configured analytics services.

How we use it

We use your information to prepare quotations, arrange travel services, respond to requests, maintain booking records and improve our website. We do not sell personal information.

Sharing and retention

When necessary to fulfil a booking, relevant details may be shared with selected hotels, transport providers, guides or other travel suppliers. Records are retained only as long as reasonably required for service, legal and accounting purposes.

Your choices

You may request access, correction or deletion of your information, subject to applicable record-keeping obligations. Contact us at {{ contact.email }}.

Security and updates

We use reasonable safeguards, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security. This policy may be updated as our services or legal obligations change.
