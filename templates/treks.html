Before you go

Trekking in Kashmir — the essentials

Carry original ID

Army checkposts on the Great Lakes and other border-area routes check every trekker. Foreign nationals need their passport and visa.

Train before you come

Moderate treks mean 6–8 hours of walking a day above 3,500 m. Regular walks, stairs and jogging for a month make a big difference.

Pick the right month

High passes open from early July and close with the first snow in late September. Meadow walks and day hikes run from spring to autumn.

Postpaid SIM only

Prepaid SIMs from outside J&K do not work here, and there is no signal on most trails. We keep your family updated from base when needed.
